GrowTube Child Safety Policy

GrowTube is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and strictly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or exploitation.

Content Source

GrowTube displays publicly available videos embedded from YouTube. While we rely on YouTube’s moderation systems, we also take responsibility for content shown within our app.

User Reporting

GrowTube provides an in-app reporting system to help users flag inappropriate or harmful content.

  • Users can report videos directly within the app
  • Each report is reviewed manually
  • Content violating policies is removed from GrowTube

Enforcement Actions

  • Removal of reported content from the platform
  • Restriction or banning of violating users

Legal Compliance

GrowTube complies with all applicable child safety laws and cooperates with relevant authorities when required.
